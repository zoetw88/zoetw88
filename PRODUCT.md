# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineers, and product builders scanning Zoe's public GitHub profile for a concise sense of how she thinks and what she builds.

## Product Purpose

Present Zoe as a curious, systems-minded backend engineer who turns difficult goals into practical, measurable feedback loops.

## Brand Personality

Curious, systems-minded, practical.

## Anti-references

- Filled mascot cartoons or detailed faces.
- Distorted human anatomy and exaggerated body movement.
- Decorative motion that competes with the profile copy.
- Generic AI-product visuals, badges, or external image services.

## Design Principles

- Keep the profile personal and concise.
- Let one restrained visual idea support the writing.
- Use evidence and systems language instead of hype.
- Preserve the recognizable stick-figure line-art identity.
- Make motion explain work in progress and completion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visuals must support GitHub light and dark themes, include accessible SVG text, remain legible at the rendered profile size, and provide a static `prefers-reduced-motion` state.
